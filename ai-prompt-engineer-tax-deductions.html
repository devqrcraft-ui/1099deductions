AI Prompt Engineer Tax Deductions 2026

As an AI Prompt Engineer or Generative AI specialist, your business relies heavily on software, hardware, and continuous learning. In 2026, the IRS allows you to deduct most ordinary and necessary expenses to reduce your taxable income.

Software & Subscriptions

Most AI-related software costs are 100% deductible as professional tools:

  • LLM Subscriptions: ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Perplexity, Gemini Advanced.
  • Image/Video Gen: Midjourney, DALL-E, Runway, Pika Labs.
  • Development Tools: GitHub Copilot, Cursor, IDE licenses.
  • API Usage: OpenAI, Anthropic, or Hugging Face API costs.

Hardware & Equipment

If you use equipment primarily for your AI business, you can write it off:

  • High-End GPUs: NVIDIA RTX series or dedicated AI accelerators.
  • Computers: MacBook Pro M3/M4 or custom-built AI workstations.
  • Monitors: Multi-monitor setups for coding and prompt testing.
  • Local Servers: NAS or home servers for running local LLMs (Llama 3, etc.).
