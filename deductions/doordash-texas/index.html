Texas State Income Tax for DoorDash Drivers

Texas does not impose a personal state income tax. DoorDash drivers in Texas may still owe federal self-employment and income taxes, and other taxes or obligations can depend on their specific activities. State-to-state comparisons require current, filing-specific calculations; this guide does not promise a fixed savings amount.

How Much Is Self-Employment Tax for a Texas DoorDash Driver in 2026?

Self-employment tax is the same in every state — it is federal, not state. On $40,000 net profit, the calculation is straightforward:

  • Net profit: $40,000
  • SE tax base (92.35%): $40,000 × 0.9235 = $36,940
  • SE tax (15.3%): $36,940 × 0.153 = $5,652
  • SE tax deduction (50%): $5,652 ÷ 2 = $2,826 off adjusted gross income

You may generally deduct the deductible portion of self-employment tax when calculating adjusted gross income. Its effect on income tax depends on your marginal rate and full return; this estimate does not promise a fixed tax saving.

"Self-employed individuals must pay self-employment tax if net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more." — IRS.gov Self-Employed Tax Center

Top Deductions for Texas DoorDash Drivers in 2026

Even without state income tax, reducing federal taxable income saves real money. These deductions are available to all 1099 DoorDash drivers regardless of state.

  1. Mileage deduction — 72.5 cents per mile (Jan–Jun) or 76 cents per mile (Jul–Dec) in 2026. Most reliable method for delivery drivers. Drive 12,000 business miles and deduct $8,700–$9,120 from gross income.
  2. Phone and data plan — business-use percentage of your monthly phone bill. If 75% of use is for DoorDash, deduct 75% of the bill plus device depreciation.
  3. Hot bags, insulated carriers, equipment — ordinary and necessary business equipment may qualify for a deduction; the timing and amount depend on business use and applicable expensing/depreciation rules.
  4. Home office — a qualifying space generally must be used regularly and exclusively for business; the deduction depends on the method and documented expenses or area.
  5. Health insurance premiums — eligible self-employed taxpayers may qualify for a deduction, subject to eligibility and income limits.
  6. Retirement plan contributions — available plans and contribution limits depend on plan type, earned income, and current IRS rules.

Quarterly Estimated Tax for Texas DoorDash Drivers

DoorDash generally does not withhold income and self-employment taxes for independent contractors. If your expected tax due meets IRS criteria, federal estimated payments may be required. Texas does not impose a personal state income tax.

  • Q1 (Jan–Mar): due April 15, 2026
  • Q2 (Apr–May): due June 15, 2026
  • Q3 (Jun–Aug): due September 15, 2026
  • Q4 (Sep–Dec): due January 15, 2027

Pay using IRS Direct Pay at irs.gov/payments — free, no account required. Alternatively, use Form 1040-ES with a check mailed to the IRS. Check IRS estimated-tax rules and safe harbors to determine whether and how much to pay.

Safe harbor rule: Pay at least 100% of last year's total federal tax (or 110% if prior-year AGI exceeded $150,000) and you avoid underpayment penalties — even if you owe more in April.

No fixed savings percentage or quarterly amount fits every DoorDash driver. Estimate federal income and self-employment tax from net profit, withholding, filing status, other income, deductions, and credits, and check current IRS estimated-tax deadlines.

How to File Taxes as a Texas DoorDash Driver: Step-by-Step

  1. Review tax forms from DoorDash when available, but report all taxable income even if no information return is issued. Form-reporting thresholds depend on current law.
  2. Total your business expenses: mileage log, phone records, receipts for equipment and supplies.
  3. Complete Schedule C (Profit or Loss from Business) — enter gross DoorDash income, subtract all deductions, report net profit.
  4. Complete Schedule SE — calculate your self-employment tax on the net profit from Schedule C.
  5. File Form 1040 with Schedule C and SE attached. Deduct half your SE tax on Schedule 1, Line 15.
  6. No state return needed. Texas does not require a state income tax return for individuals.
"Self-employed individuals must report all income from gig platforms on Schedule C, regardless of whether a 1099-NEC was issued." — IRS.gov

Frequently Asked Questions

How much SE tax does a DoorDash driver pay in Texas on $40,000?

On $40,000 net profit, a DoorDash driver in Texas owes $5,652 in self-employment tax. Texas has no state income tax, so there is no additional state tax burden on DoorDash earnings.

Does Texas have a state income tax on DoorDash earnings?

Texas does not impose a personal state income tax. DoorDash drivers may still owe federal self-employment and income taxes, and their total liability depends on their full tax situation. No fixed savings amount applies to every driver.

What mileage deduction can Texas DoorDash drivers claim in 2026?

The IRS business mileage rate is 72.5 cents per mile from January 1 through June 30, 2026, and 76 cents per mile from July 1 through December 31. Only qualified business miles may be claimed, and a mileage log is important. The tax effect depends on your full return.

When are quarterly tax payments due for Texas DoorDash drivers?

Federal quarterly estimated tax due dates in 2026: April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15, 2027. Texas has no state estimated tax requirement — only federal Form 1040-ES must be filed each quarter.

How much does a DoorDash driver keep after taxes in Texas vs California?

Texas does not impose a personal state income tax, but comparing after-tax income across states requires state-specific calculations and personal filing details. No fixed savings amount applies to every driver.

Can DoorDash drivers in Texas deduct the phone bill?

Yes. The business-use percentage of your phone is deductible. If you use your phone 80% for DoorDash, deduct 80% of the monthly bill and the prorated cost of the device over its useful life.

Do DoorDash drivers in Texas need to collect sales tax?

Whether sales-tax registration or collection applies depends on the transaction, platform arrangements, and applicable Texas rules. Check official Texas Comptroller guidance and your platform's instructions rather than assuming every driver's obligations are identical.
